Is this job worth applying to?

Paste your resume and the job ad. You get the honest answer: what you match, what you are missing, and which requirement would actually stop you. Your resume stays on this device — nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and no AI is involved.

JobPilot never submits anything and never fills a form for you. It reads what you paste, on your device. Skills are matched through an alias dictionary, so “k8s” and “Kubernetes” count as the same thing — but that dictionary is not exhaustive, and a skill it does not know will show as missing. Check the lists before trusting them.
